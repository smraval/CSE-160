greetings and welcome to my blocky world 🦀

꩜ overview: 
- animated crabs: 5 animate crabs (my blocky animal) hidden across the world
- crabs face you: each crab rotates on Y to always face the camera
- find-the-crabs game: walk within 3 units of a crab and shift+click to catch it
- crab counter: on-screen crabs found display
- neutral camera angle checkbox: locks vertical look so the camera stays level
- FPS counter: live frames-per-second display 

꩜ rubric features overview: 
- ground: flat textured plane made from a scaled cube
- sky box: giant cube filled with solid blue
- textured walls: all wall cubes use wall.jpg
- textured ground: ground uses ground.jpg 
- WASD movement: W/S move forward/backward, A/D left/right
- Q/E rotation: turn camera left and right (also via ← →)
- mouse drag rotation: drag to look around 
- add blocks: left click places a block in front of you
- delete blocks: right click removes the top block in front of you
